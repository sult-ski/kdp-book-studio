# PRODUCT

## Who uses it
Self-publishing authors and small-press formatters preparing a KDP paperback. Mostly non-technical, often on a phone, often first-time publishers. The owner also uses it on real books.

## Their goal
Know, before uploading to Amazon KDP, whether the interior PDF and cover will be accepted, and exactly what to change if not. One file in, one answer out.

## Reading level and context
- Plain English, roughly age 12+. No print-industry jargon without a one-line explanation.
- Anxious moment: a rejected upload costs days. They want a clear verdict first, details second.
- Many are older or reading in a second language: large base text, high contrast, short sentences.

## Trust needs
- Privacy is the first thing they ask: the PDF never leaves the device. Say it briefly, everywhere it matters.
- Every claim is honest: `verified` vs `best-effort` is shown; unsupported checks are listed, never faked.
- Our own rules (for example the 200 DPI "very low" line) are labelled as ours, not KDP's.
- Sources (KDP help pages) are one click away.

## Tone in 3 words
Calm. Plain. Honest.

## Not
A marketing site, a dashboard, or a developer tool. No hype, no badges for their own sake, no alarm colours unless KDP would actually reject the file.
