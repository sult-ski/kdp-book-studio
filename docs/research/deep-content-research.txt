KDP Deep Content Check: Evidence-Based Rules for a Browser-Side Manuscript
Scanner
Text rules alone can reliably catch the issues that Amazon most clearly rejects in a manuscript: placeholder text (lorem ipsum), Project Gutenberg
boilerplate, links to or mentions of other ebook stores, leftover AI chatbot text, bonus content placed at the front, and duplicated passages. Other big
causes of blocks, including undifferentiated public-domain text, text that is freely available on the web, copyright and lyrics, and erotica rules, can only be
partly detected, so the checker should raise warnings for these, not give a pass or fail.


TL;DR
   Highest-value checks backed by official rules: placeholder text (KDP's print guidelines say "We will reject any files with placeholder text, such as Lorem
   Ipsum"); media-amazon links to "commercial eBook store sites other than Amazon"; Gutenberg license and header text, which is a sign of
   undifferentiated public-domain content; bonus content placed before the main content; duplicated chapters or passages; and companion guides or
   summaries.
   AI text: KDP requires you to disclose AI-generated text, images and translations in the dashboard, and a text scanner can't check that box for you.
   undetectable amazon Leftover chatbot phrases have appeared in named published books: Lena McDonald's Darkhollow Academy: Year 2, chapter 3
   ("I've rewritten the passage to align more with J. Bree's style…", per 404 Media, May 23, 2025), and K.C. Crowne's Dark Obsession ("Thought for 13
   seconds. Here's an advanced version of your passage…", per the Daily Dot and Slashdot's summary of 404 Media). Rania Faris's Rogue Souls was also
   named. These caused reader backlash. No source shows KDP itself removing a book because of them.
   Weak or unknown evidence (warn only): words like "Kindle Unlimited" or "Amazon" inside the interior (KDP's official trademark limits apply to metadata
   fields, and no interior-enforcement case was found); review requests in back matter (polite requests are reported as fine, incentives are not); single lines
   of song lyrics (the risk is legal, and documented KDP blocks are thin).


Key Findings

the rule covers metadata (title, description, keywords) rather than the interior, the table says so. The checker can scan metadata too if the user pastes it in.

Details
How enforcement works (official). KDP says it uses "a combination of machine learning, automation, and dedicated teams of human reviewers" and will
"reject or remove content that does not adhere to these guidelines." cleanpagepress amazon amazon The Content Quality guide divides problems into
Critical (removed from sale until fixed), Destructive and Distracting issues. amazon Repeat violations can affect your account. Content rejection emails
reported on KDP Community warn that further similar submissions may lead to the account being "terminated or you may lose access to optional KDP
services." goodreads book-business-kdp-amazon

Public domain is the clearest case where a content problem can lead to account suspension. In a 2012 Warrior Forum thread, user "Calamaroo" wrote: "I
had 70 ebooks on Amazon Kindle. Ten of these used public domain works that I got from Project Gutenberg." The account was first "temporarily blocked"
because those works were not "differentiated", then terminated even after the poster removed the ten. This is a single, unverified account, but it matches
KDP's official PD rules. Gutenberg boilerplate in a manuscript is the strongest text signal that the edition is undifferentiated. Gutenberg's own license also
requires you to remove it if you sell the text. omeka gutenberg

AI leftovers cause reputation damage; KDP enforcement is not documented. In the Lena McDonald case (Darkhollow Academy: Year 2), the leftover
prompt text was later removed from the Amazon version. 404 Media reported: "Searching for it on the site won't show the book, but a Google search will.
404 Media was able to purchase a copy and confirm that the book no longer contains the reference to copying Bree's style." 404 Media did not confirm that
KDP removed it. Treat this as a check that protects quality and reputation, and pair it with a reminder about disclosure.

Freely available content is checked by bots, but the browser can't check the web. kdpcommunity KDP Community reports quote KDP's email: "we found
content that is freely available on the web… please provide… An explanation as to why the content is available online." kdpcommunity A community
member noted that "KDP usually gives five days to clear up the problem." kdpcommunity Client-side, the checker can only catch self-declared sources
(Wattpad, blog, PLR).

Kindle Unlimited is now Kindle+. writtenwordmedia The KDP Help navigation now shows "Kindle+" in place of Kindle Unlimited. amazon amazon Any
trademark or exclusivity rule should match both names.


Recommendations — Top 15 checks ranked by impact (likelihood of block/unpublish/suspension ×
detectability)
 1. Project Gutenberg header/footer/license text: PD differentiation rule plus account-level risk; regex is exact. warriorforum amazon Block-level warning.
 2. Placeholder text (lorem ipsum, [Insert…], TODO, Chapter X): officially rejected; near-perfect detection. media-amazon publishing
 3. Links to non-Amazon ebook stores (Apple, Kobo, B&N, Google Play, Smashwords, D2D, Books2Read): officially prohibited; URL regex is exact.
    ebookpbook amazon
 4. Front-loaded bonus content / previews before Chapter 1, or bonus >~10%: a Critical Issue; detect by position and ratio. amazon amazon
 5. Duplicated chapters/paragraphs: Critical when mislabeled; paragraph hashing is reliable.
 6. Leftover AI chatbot text + disclosure reminder: frequent, damaging, exact phrases.
 7. Freely-available/PLR source declarations (Wattpad, Royal Road, "originally published on", PLR/resell rights): documented blocks; partial detection.
 8. Companion guide/summary patterns: officially "not allowed" with narrow exceptions.
 9. Redirect/incomplete content ("to continue reading…", QR to full text): Critical when main content is external.
10. Erotica taboo co-occurrence (sexual terms + minor/family/non-consent/bestiality terms): high severity; partial detection, so route to human review.
11. Children's-category + explicit/violent lexicon: official category ineligibility; easy lexicon check once the user picks the audience.
12. Incentivized review language (5-star, gift card, "in exchange for a review"): official review-policy violation; exact phrases. book-business-kdp-amazon
13. Newsletter/web-form links: officially listed as prohibited links; enforcement frequency unknown, so warn.
14. Metadata mismatch (manuscript title/ISBN vs. entered metadata; Amazon/Kindle/KDP in the publisher line): official; needs user input.
15. Song lyrics / long in-copyright quotations: legal and KDP permission-request risk; low-precision heuristics, so make it an advisory only.

Implementation notes: Let users mark context (genre, PD-annotated edition, AI-themed book, typography book, hymnal or liturgy, KDP Select yes/no,
children's audience), since these settings remove most false positives. Show every flag with its evidence level and the source URL. Never auto-fail on
"partly" checks.


Caveats
   Unknown: a minimum length for "too short"; any interior-text enforcement against "Kindle Unlimited"/"Amazon" mentions (no case found); how often
   newsletter-link and plain-text store mentions are actually enforced; whether KDP itself removed any of the AI-prompt books.
   Some "reported" sources are SEO-oriented 2026 blogs (e.g., formatmyebook.com, cruxone.io) or older forum anecdotes; they are listed as corroboration
   only, never as the sole basis for a hard block.
   The placeholder rule comes from KDP's 2018 Print Publishing Guidelines PDF; current ebook pages don't repeat it word for word, though "missing
   content" and quality rules cover it. media-amazon
   The 10% KDP Select sample allowance comes from the old KDP Select FAQ (as quoted on Goodreads in 2014: "you may choose to make up to 10% of
   your book available on other sites as a sample") and a 2012 support email, not the current Terms.
   Rules change; re-check the cited KDP Help pages before each release of the checker.
